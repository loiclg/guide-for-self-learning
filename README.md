# A Guide to Self-Learning in the Age of AI

This document is a guide to effective self-directed learning. It gathers best practices, mainly drawn from Dr. Oakley’s online course and supplemented by others, organized step by step to support personal learning.

In the age of AI, learning with agility is a key skill for keeping pace with the rapid evolution of practices and professions. Managing your own learning is thus as essential as being able to organize yourself or communicate effectively about your work.

The guide provides a clear framework to serve as a reference and share best practices, including ways AI can enhance learning. It is designed to be evolving, so it can be adapted and improved continuously based on experience and feedback.
